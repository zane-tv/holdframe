# video-skills

Skill pack for educational motion videos. No speech synthesis.

- Do not add voice packs, TTS models, speaker IDs, or a voiceover step to these skills.
- `youtube-explainer` is the production workflow. Timing comes from the storyboard, not from measured narration.
- `motion-design`, `review-loop`, `sound-design`, and `web-sims` are instruction skills from [whaleyxbt/claude-motion](https://github.com/whaleyxbt/claude-motion). Their SFX engine and sim capture scripts are not vendored here.
- Videos a consumer produces belong in that workspace's `projects/<slug>/`, not in this repository.
